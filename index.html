About Nostra Express

Providing reliable clearing, forwarding, and global logistics solutions.

Nostra Express started with a single goal: to provide the most reliable, fast, and secure logistics, clearing, and forwarding services. Over the years, we have built a reputation of trust, ensuring that our clients' cargo always gets to its destination safely and without delay.

We are primarily based at Nnamdi Azikiwe International Airport, Abuja, but we also carry out clearing operations at Murtala Muhammed International Airport (MMIA) in Lagos, and Aminu Kano International Airport in Kano. We ensure seamless import and export operations, including the safe handling of pets, live animals, and human remains, alongside professional customs brokerage, secure warehousing, and heavy haulage.
